About

Reading landscapes, translating them.

I'm a geospatial scientist working across ArcGIS, Python, and remote sensing — with a soft spot for what small unmanned aircraft can tell us about the land underneath them. My work usually sits between raw spatial data and the people trying to act on it: carbon estimation from drone imagery, Python tools that speed up GIS teams, and getting spatial answers in front of decisions when they need to be made.

I hold a Master's in GIS from CSU Long Beach and a Bachelor's in Geography & GIS from CSU Sacramento. I've volunteered with URISA GISCorps since 2021, and I'm currently paying close attention to where agentic AI meets geospatial workflows.

Off a screen, I'm in the landscape itself.
